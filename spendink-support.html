SpendInk Support

Welcome to the SpendInk Support page. If you need assistance, have questions, or want to provide feedback, you can reach out in several ways.

Contact Email

For direct support, please email us at: infospendink@gmail.com

To help us answer faster, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Please never send us your passcode or exported backup files unless we ask for them.

In-App Feedback

You can also reach us directly from within the app:
Tools → Feedback
This allows you to send feedback or report issues easily from inside SpendInk. Your name and email are optional; add your email if you would like a reply.

Common Questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open Tools → Settings (gear icon) → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy Premium.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → SpendInk. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I back up my data or move it to a new iPhone?

Your data is stored only on your device. Use Tools → Export data to save an encrypted backup file, then open SpendInk on the new device and use Tools → Import data to restore it. Your passcode and Face ID settings are not included in backups.

How do I delete all my data?

Use Tools → Clear all data (at the bottom of the screen), or uninstall the app. Clearing data cannot be undone, so export a backup first if you might need it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where available in your region, open Tools → Settings (gear icon) → Ad privacy choices. You can also control tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Premium removes all ads.
