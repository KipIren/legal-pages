Common questions

How much does Splilit cost?

Your first split is free, and joining someone else’s split is always free. After that, each new split you create is a one-time purchase, or you can subscribe to Splilit Unlimited (monthly or yearly) to create as many splits as you like.

How do I invite friends to a split?

Open the split and share its invite link. Anyone with the link can join, so share it only with the people in the split. If a link gets out, make a new one: the old one stops working and people already in stay in.

How do I cancel or manage Splilit Unlimited?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Splilit. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Unlimited until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Sign in to Splilit, open Account → Plans, and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

I didn’t get the confirmation or password-reset email.

Check your Spam or Junk folder. Email links don’t work while the message is in Spam, so move it to your Inbox first, then tap the button.

Where did my receipt photo go?

Receipt photos are kept for 1 month in a free split, and for 1 year in a bought split or with Splilit Unlimited. After that the photo is deleted, and the expense keeps its items and amounts.

How do I keep a copy of a split?

Open the split, tap the menu at the top and choose Export to save a PDF report or a CSV spreadsheet.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Account → Delete account. See Delete your account for what is deleted and what stays for the others in your splits.
