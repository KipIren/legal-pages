Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 12, 2026

Splilit is an app for splitting shared expenses with friends. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. It applies to the Splilit iOS app and these web pages.

In short: you need an account so that the people in your splits can see the same expenses. We store your account and your splits with our hosting provider. Receipts are read on your phone. Payments go through Apple. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your information.

1. Who we are

Splilit (“we”, “us”) is responsible for the information described here (the “data controller”). Contact: splilitinkinfo@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Your account

  • Email address and password, if you sign up with email. Passwords are stored by our sign-in provider only as a one-way hash; we never see them.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google: your email address (with Apple this can be a private relay address if you choose “Hide My Email”) and, if you share it, your name. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile: the name you show to others, an optional profile photo, your default currency and your app language.

2.2 Your splits

  • Split details: name, type, dates, currency and an optional cover photo.
  • The people in a split: the names you or other members enter. People can be added by name only, without an account of their own.
  • Expenses: description, amount, currency, date, category icon, who paid and how it is shared.
  • Payments recorded when people settle up, and an activity history of changes in the split.
  • Invite links you create to let others join a split.

2.3 Receipt photos

When you scan a receipt, the text is read on your phone (by Apple’s Vision framework, or for Hebrew receipts by a text-recognition model built into the app). The photo is not sent anywhere to be read.

If you attach the receipt photo to an expense, the photo is uploaded to private storage so that the other members of that split can see it. Receipt photos are kept for a limited time, counted from when they were added:

  • 1 month in a split that was created for free;
  • 1 year in a split that was bought, or whose creator has Splilit Unlimited.

After that, the photo is deleted automatically and the expense keeps everything else (the deletion runs in the background and can take a day or two).

2.4 Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your card or bank details. To give you what you bought, the app sends us Apple’s signed record of the purchase, and we store the product, Apple’s transaction ID, the purchase date, the subscription’s expiry date and whether it was refunded or revoked, linked to your account.

2.5 Feedback

The app may ask you to rate it. If you give 1 to 3 stars, you can send us a short message; we store the rating, your message and your account ID. Ratings of 4 or 5 stars go to Apple’s App Store rating prompt instead, which Apple handles.

2.6 Information stored only on your phone

Your sign-in session, theme, language, recently used currencies, cached exchange rates and whether you have seen the welcome screen or rating prompt are stored on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

2.7 What we do not collect

No advertising identifiers, no location, no contacts, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

3. How we use information

  • To create your account, sign you in and send sign-in emails (confirming your address, resetting your password).
  • To store your splits and show them to the people you share them with, and to calculate balances and who owes whom.
  • To check and deliver your purchases and subscription.
  • To answer your messages and improve the app using feedback you send.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decisions, and we do not sell or rent it.

4. Who can see your information

  • Members of a split see everything in that split: its details, member names, expenses, payments, activity, photos and receipt photos. They also see the name and profile photo of people they share a split with.
  • Anyone with an invite link can join that split and then see it as a member. Share links only with people you trust; the split’s members can reset the link at any time.
  • Files you export (PDF or CSV reports of a split) are created on your phone and shared only where you choose.

6. International transfers

Our providers may store and process information in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Receipt photos: 1 month or 1 year, as described in section 2.3, or sooner if the expense, the photo or the split is deleted.
  • Splits: until the person who created the split deletes it. A split is shared with its other members, so it is not deleted when one member deletes their account (see section 8).
  • Purchase records: until you delete your account.
  • Feedback: kept to improve the app; when you delete your account it is no longer linked to you.

Deleted information can remain in our hosting provider’s routine backups for a short time until they are replaced.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Account → Delete account. This deletes your account, profile, profile photo and purchase records at once. Splits you share with others stay for them, with your name on past expenses. See Delete your account for the full details.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Splilit Unlimited subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent. You can do most of this in the app: edit your profile and splits, export a split as PDF or CSV, and delete your account. For anything else, email splilitinkinfo@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland

We rely on these legal bases: contract (providing the account, splits and purchases you asked for); legitimate interests (keeping the service secure, improving it from feedback); legal obligation (for example, keeping records the law requires).

California

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information beyond what is needed to provide the app. You have the right to know, delete and correct your information, and we will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

10. Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). Your data is protected by access rules so that only you and the members of your splits can read it. Photos are stored privately and shown only through links that expire after an hour. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

11. Children

Splilit is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will let you know in the app or by email before it takes effect.
