Delete your account

Last updated: November 12, 2026

In the app

  1. Open Splilit and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out. This cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you can’t use the app, email splilitinkinfo@gmail.com from the address you signed up with (or tell us that address if you used Sign in with Apple with “Hide My Email”). We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

Have a subscription? Deleting your account does not cancel Splilit Unlimited. Cancel it first in your Apple ID settings: on iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Splilit.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in: email address, password, and any link to Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Your profile: name, email address, profile photo, default currency and language.
  • Your purchase records: unused split purchases and your subscription record. Unused split purchases are lost.
  • Your access to every split you were in.

What stays for the other people in your splits

A split belongs to everyone in it, so deleting your account does not delete shared splits or rewrite their history:

  • Splits you created stay for their other members.
  • In each split you were in, your name stays on past expenses and payments, as a name without an account, so the balances still add up.
  • Expenses, payments, split photos and receipt photos you added stay in the split. Receipt photos are still deleted when their time is up (1 month, or 1 year in a bought split).

Want a split gone completely? Before deleting your account, delete the splits you created (open the split → the menu at the top → Delete split). This removes the split and everything in it for everyone. Only the person who created a split can delete it. To get a copy first, export the split as a PDF or CSV.

Other information

  • Feedback you sent from the app is kept, but no longer linked to you.
  • Settings stored on your phone are removed when you delete the app.
  • Deleted information can remain in our hosting provider’s routine backups for a short time until they are replaced.
