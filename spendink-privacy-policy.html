PRIVACY POLICY FOR SPENDINK EXPENSE TRACKER

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SpendInk ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

1. OVERVIEW

SpendInk is designed to respect and protect user privacy. Your financial data is stored only on your device. The app does not require a user account, and we do not run servers that store your financial data. A small amount of information is handled by third-party services (advertising, exchange rates, purchases) and, only if you choose to send it, by our feedback form. This policy explains each case.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 FINANCIAL DATA (STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE)

  • Expense and income entries
  • Wallets, account balances and related calculations
  • Custom categories
  • Savings and budgeting information
  • Recurring transaction settings
  • Currency conversion selections
  • Budget configurations

2.2 USER PREFERENCES (STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE)

  • Language selection
  • Theme and appearance settings
  • Preferred currency
  • Notification and reminder settings
  • Quick-action configurations
  • Subscription (Premium) status

2.3 SECURITY SETTINGS (STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE)

  • Your passcode, stored only as a salted one-way hash (the passcode itself is never stored)
  • Whether Face ID / Touch ID unlock is turned on
  • App access protection settings

Face ID and Touch ID are handled entirely by iOS. SpendInk never receives, stores or transmits your biometric data; it only learns whether the check succeeded.

2.4 INFORMATION YOU CHOOSE TO SEND US (FEEDBACK)

If you use the in-app feedback form, the following is sent to us:

  • Your feedback message
  • Your name (optional)
  • Your email address (optional)
  • The app version

Feedback is transmitted over a secure (HTTPS) connection and stored using Google services (Google Apps Script). Sending feedback is entirely optional. No financial data is ever included.

2.5 INFORMATION COLLECTED BY THIRD PARTIES

The app uses third-party services that may collect limited information necessary for their functionality.

a) Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) - free version only

  • Device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking)
  • IP address and general device information
  • Ad interaction and performance data

Ads are shown only in the free version. Premium users see no ads, and no ad data is collected for them.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

b) Exchange Rate API (exchangerate-api.com)

  • Currency codes for rate retrieval
  • The technical data that any internet request carries, such as your IP address

No financial data or personally identifiable information from the app is sent.

c) Apple In-App Purchases

  • Information required to process transactions

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

3.1 FINANCIAL DATA

  • Display and manage financial entries
  • Generate summaries, insights, and analytics (calculated on your device)
  • Support savings and budgeting features
  • Process recurring transactions
  • Provide currency conversion functionality

3.2 USER PREFERENCES

  • Customize app appearance and behavior
  • Deliver reminder notifications (scheduled locally on your device)
  • Preserve app settings across updates

3.3 SECURITY SETTINGS

  • Protect access to the application
  • Verify authentication using passcode or biometrics

3.4 FEEDBACK

  • Read and respond to your questions, suggestions and bug reports
  • Improve the app

3.5 THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

  • Deliver advertisements (free version)
  • Retrieve currency exchange information
  • Process in-app purchases

4. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

4.1 LOCAL STORAGE

  • All financial and preference data is stored in a database on your device
  • The database is protected by iOS data protection and is not readable while your device is locked
  • Access protections rely on your device's security features

4.2 NO CLOUD STORAGE BY SPENDINK

  • We do not maintain servers for storing your financial information, and the app does not sync it to any online service
  • Like other app data, your SpendInk data may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup, if you have enabled it. These backups are created and managed by Apple under your Apple account settings

4.3 EXPORTED BACKUP FILES

Backup files you export (.sib) are encrypted so they cannot be read as plain text. However, any copy of SpendInk can restore them, so please store and share exported files only with people and places you trust. Exported files never include your passcode or Face ID settings.

4.4 SECURITY MEASURES

  • Optional passcode protection
  • Optional Face ID / Touch ID authentication
  • App content hidden in the app switcher while passcode protection is on
  • OS-level sandboxing and data isolation
  • Secure purchase processing via Apple
  • Encrypted (HTTPS) connections for all network requests

5. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

5.1 GENERAL POLICY

We do not sell or trade personal information. Financial data remains stored on your device.

5.2 LIMITED SHARING

Information shared with third parties is limited to:

  • Advertising and device data for AdMob (free version only)
  • Currency codes for exchange rate retrieval
  • Transaction details for in-app purchases (handled by Apple)
  • Feedback you choose to send, stored using Google services

5.3 LEGAL REQUIREMENTS

We may disclose information if required to comply with legal obligations, though we hold very little data that could be subject to such requests (only feedback you have sent us).

5.4 USER-INITIATED SHARING

The app allows you to export your data. Any sharing of exported files is fully controlled by you.

6.3 DELETION

  • Changing your choice: Where applicable, an "Ad privacy choices" option in the app's settings lets you change your consent at any time.
  • No ads: Upgrading to Premium removes all ads.
